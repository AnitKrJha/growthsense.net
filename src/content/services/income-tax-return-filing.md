  - heading: Basics

process:
  - step: Share what you have
    detail: Send a message on WhatsApp or call. You will get a checklist that fits your situation, and a quote.
  - step: Documents and review
    detail: Share the documents. Your income, TDS and deductions are cross-checked against AIS and Form 26AS.
  - step: Approve the summary
    detail: You receive a plain-language summary of income, deductions, tax and refund under both regimes. Nothing is filed until you approve it.

timeline: Most straightforward salaried or pension returns can be prepared within a couple of working days of receiving complete documents. Returns with capital gains, business income or mismatches in AIS take longer. Please don't wait for the last week before the due date.
faqs:
  - question: What is the due date for filing my ITR?
    answer: For most individuals who don't need a tax audit, the due date is usually 31 July after the end of the financial year. Other cases have later dates, and the government sometimes extends them. The current due date for your case will be confirmed when you get in touch.
  - question: Which regime should I choose, old or new?
    answer: It depends on how much you can claim in deductions such as 80C, 80D, HRA and home-loan interest. The new regime is the default and has lower rates but few deductions. Your return is worked out both ways so you can see the difference. You can also try the <a href="/tools/income-tax-calculator">income tax calculator</a>.
  - question: My employer already deducted TDS. Do I still need to file?
    answer: Often, yes. TDS is tax paid in advance; the return is where your final tax is worked out and any refund is claimed. Filing is compulsory above certain income levels and in some other situations, and it is useful as proof of income for loans and visas.
  - question: I missed the due date. Can I still file?
    answer: In many cases a belated or updated return can still be filed within the time limits allowed by law, sometimes with a late fee or additional tax. Send a message with the year concerned and it will be checked.
  - question: Can you help with an income tax notice?
    answer: Help is available for understanding common intimations and notices and preparing a response on the portal. The exact scope depends on the notice. Please share a copy (not your password) so it can be assessed first.

Filing your income tax return is not hard when the numbers are right, and getting them right is the part that needs care. Salary from two employers, interest you forgot about, a mutual fund you redeemed or a TDS entry that doesn't match can all lead to a notice later.

This service takes you from "what do I need?" to a filed and e-verified return. Everything is checked against what the income tax department already knows about you (Form 26AS and AIS), and you see a clear summary before anything is filed.
