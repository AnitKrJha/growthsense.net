faqs:
  - question: I got an income tax notice. Should I panic?
    answer: Usually not. Many notices are automated intimations or requests for a small correction. What matters is reading it carefully and replying before the due date. Share it and you'll get a plain-English explanation first.
  - question: Can you guarantee the demand will be dropped?
    answer: No. The outcome is decided by the Income Tax Department. What you can expect is a careful check of the facts and a clear, well-documented reply.
  - question: Why hasn't my refund arrived?
    answer: Common reasons are a bank account that isn't pre-validated on the income tax portal, a PAN-Aadhaar linking problem, or the refund being adjusted against an older demand. The refund status on the portal usually shows which one.
  - question: How do I know a notice is genuine?
    answer: Genuine notices can be verified on the income tax portal using the document identification number (DIN). Never pay money to anyone claiming to settle a notice over the phone.

An income tax notice in your inbox is unsettling, but most of them come down to a mismatch that can be explained or corrected. The same goes for refunds that seem stuck.

This service helps you understand what the department is asking, find the cause, and reply properly and on time. Refund problems are followed up until they are resolved. Nothing is filed without your approval, and no outcome is promised.
