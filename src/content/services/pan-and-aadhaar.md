faqs:
  - question: My PAN says "inoperative". What does that mean?
    answer: It usually means the PAN hasn't been linked with Aadhaar. An inoperative PAN can cause problems with refunds, higher TDS and some financial transactions. Linking it (after paying any applicable fee on the official portal) usually makes it operative again.
  - question: Is there a fee?
    answer: An instant e-PAN via Aadhaar is free on the income tax portal. Other applications and some linking requests carry a government fee, which you pay directly on the official portal. The fee for this help is quoted separately, upfront.
  - question: Should I share my Aadhaar here?
    answer: Please don't send Aadhaar or PAN through the website form. Share them over WhatsApp or in person when asked, only for the work you want done.

A PAN problem tends to surface at the worst moment: a refund that won't credit, a bank asking for KYC, or a property deal that needs it tomorrow.

This service covers the everyday PAN and Aadhaar tasks: new PAN, corrections, reprints and PAN-Aadhaar linking. Everything is done through official portals, and any government fee is paid by you directly.
