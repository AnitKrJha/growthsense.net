timeline: A registration application can usually be prepared within a working day or two of receiving complete documents. faqs:
  - question: Do I need GST registration?
    answer: Registration is compulsory once your turnover crosses the limit that applies to your state and to whether you supply goods or services. It is also required in some cases regardless of turnover, such as certain inter-state or e-commerce sales. Your situation will be checked before you apply.
  - question: What are GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?
    answer: GSTR-1 reports your sales invoice by invoice. GSTR-3B is a summary return where you pay the tax due after claiming input tax credit. Both are filed monthly, or quarterly under the QRMP scheme if you are eligible.
  - question: What happens if I file late?
    answer: Late filing attracts a late fee per day and interest on any tax paid late. Continued non-filing can lead to your GSTIN being suspended or cancelled, so it is worth keeping up even when there are no sales (a nil return).
  - question: Can I work out GST on a price myself?
    answer: Yes. The <a href="/tools/gst-calculator">GST calculator</a> adds or removes GST and splits it into CGST and SGST or IGST.

GST work is mostly about routine: the right registration at the start, then clean returns filed on time every month or quarter. Missed dates mean late fees and interest, and mismatched invoices mean lost input tax credit.

This service covers both parts. It starts with checking whether you actually need to register and which scheme suits you. After that, your GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are prepared from your invoices, reconciled with GSTR-2B and filed, with a reminder before every due date.
