# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in India who need income tax, GST or TDS work done and want one competent, reachable person to handle it:

- salaried employees and pensioners filing an ITR
- small shop owners and traders who need GST registration or monthly returns
- freelancers and small businesses with TDS obligations
- anyone who needs help paying a tax or vehicle challan on an official portal

Scene: a shop owner or salaried employee in an Indian city opens a WhatsApp-forwarded link on a mid-range Android phone in the evening. They are a little anxious about a due date or a notice, and they are deciding in about ten seconds whether this person looks competent, legitimate and easy to reach.

## Product Purpose

The site is the personal practice site of **Avinash Thakur**, trading as **GrowthSense**. It exists to turn that anxious visitor into a WhatsApp message, a call or an enquiry. The free calculators build trust and bring in search traffic. Success means more first messages, and messages that arrive with the right context (which service, which documents).

## Brand Personality

**Calm, sharp, human.** GrowthSense is the neighbourhood tax person who is also unusually good at it: organised, precise, never flashy about money, and warm in tone. The emotional arc is *mess → order → relief*: you hand over a pile of confusing paperwork and get back an acknowledgement.

Voice:
- plain English, short sentences, and no jargon without an explanation
- confident but never boastful
- honest about what isn't promised (refund amounts, fine waivers, loan approvals)

## Anti-references

- Generic CA-firm templates: navy and gold, stock handshake photos, "Trusted by 500+ clients" counters, laurel badges.
- SaaS-landing clichés: gradient blobs, hero-metric stat rows, identical icon-card grids, glassmorphism.
- Crypto or fintech neon on black.
- Government-portal clutter: dense link farms, tiny type, marquee banners.
- Editorial-magazine affectation: italic display serifs, rule-separated columns, tiny tracked labels everywhere.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the service, don't describe it.** The metaphor is literal: papers in, acknowledgement out. Motion and 3D exist to tell that story, never as decoration.
2. **Spectacle on the landing page, composure everywhere else.** The home hero may be ambitious. Service pages, calculators, forms and CTAs are calm, legible and official. Nobody should hunt for the WhatsApp button.
3. **Useful before persuasive.** Live deadlines, real calculators and exact document checklists earn trust faster than claims.
4. **Honest by construction.** No invented stats, testimonials or credentials. Placeholders are visible until the owner confirms. Estimates are always labelled.
5. **Fast on a cheap phone.** Heavy effects load after the content, degrade gracefully, and switch off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: semantic HTML, labelled form fields, visible focus, 44px tap targets, AA contrast on every text/background pair.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support. The 3D hero becomes a static composed image, and scroll stories become plain stacked content.
- Hindi may be added later (`/hi/` routes). Type choices must have a Devanagari companion (Anek Devanagari).
- Number formatting is Indian (₹12,00,000).
